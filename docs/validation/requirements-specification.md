46 requirements in 10 areas. Each is traced to the automated tests that verify it in the [traceability matrix](traceability-matrix.md) and rated in the [risk assessment](risk-assessment.md).

Scope: the LabVault application and its production container (ADR 0024), for managing QC samples, test results, reviews and OOS investigations.

## Access control

| ID | Requirement | Regulation |
|---|---|---|
| ACC-01 | Each user signs in with a unique account and a password stored only as a salted, slow hash. | 21 CFR 11.100(a); 21 CFR 11.300(a); Annex 11 §12.1 |
| ACC-02 | Repeated failed sign-ins lock the account for a set time, without revealing whether the account exists. | 21 CFR 11.300(d); Annex 11 §12.1 |
| ACC-03 | An idle session is signed out after the configured time. | 21 CFR 11.10(d); Annex 11 §12.1 |
| ACC-04 | Only administrators reach administration pages and actions (accounts, specifications, closing investigations). | 21 CFR 11.10(d); 21 CFR 11.10(g); Annex 11 §12.2 |
| ACC-05 | Outside demo mode there are no default or published credentials; the initial admin password is set by the operator. | 21 CFR 11.300(b); Annex 11 §12.1 |
| ACC-06 | Account changes (create, reset, unlock, disable, role) are made by an admin, with a reason, and audited; temporary passwords must be replaced at first sign-in. | 21 CFR 11.10(d); 21 CFR 11.300(b); Annex 11 §12.3 |
| ACC-07 | A disabled account cannot sign in or change data, and its open session ends at the next interaction. | 21 CFR 11.10(d); Annex 11 §12.3 |
| ACC-08 | System accounts (import, simulator, migrations) can never sign in, sign or be assigned work. | 21 CFR 11.10(d); 21 CFR 11.100(a) |

## Results and specifications

| ID | Requirement | Regulation |
|---|---|---|

## OOS investigations

| ID | Requirement | Regulation |
|---|---|---|
| OOS-01 | Every out-of-specification result opens an investigation automatically. | 21 CFR 211.192; FDA OOS Guidance (2022) |
| OOS-02 | An OOS result cannot be signed while its investigation is open; the conclusion decides the signature's meaning. | 21 CFR 211.192; FDA OOS Guidance (2022) |
| OOS-03 | An investigation is closed by an admin who is not the analyst, with a conclusion, findings and password; once closed it is final. | 21 CFR 211.192; 21 CFR 11.200(a) |
