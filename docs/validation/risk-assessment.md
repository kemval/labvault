# LabVault risk assessment

> **DRAFT — not an executed validation.** Generated by `scripts/traceability.py` from `docs/validation/requirements.toml`; edit that file, not this one. What a regulated lab must still do is in [the validation plan](validation-plan.md).

Method: GAMP 5 (2nd ed.) quality risk management. For each requirement, the **impact** of a failure on product quality, patient safety or data integrity and its **likelihood** without the listed controls give the **risk class** (1 highest). The class and the **detectability** of a failure before harm give the **priority**, which sets how much verification a lab should require (see the validation plan).
